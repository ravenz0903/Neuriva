[ Clinical Protocol ]

Scope, compliance and Phase 2 milestones.

The engine states its operating envelope explicitly. Territory scoring is a geometric heuristic on a single ganglionic axial level; it is not a substitute for volumetric perfusion assessment or expert interpretation.

Protocol Compliance
Topographic evaluation aligned with AHA/ASA Acute Ischemic Stroke guidelines and the Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score template for the ganglionic level.
Detection Method
Otsu parenchymal isolation with bone exclusion, symmetry-optimised midline registration, and contralateral attenuation differencing against the mirrored hemisphere. Territories are normalized to the segmented parenchyma and swept along its measured radial contour, so scoring is stable across head position, field of view and matrix size.
Stated Limitations
Supraganglionic cortical territories (M4, M5, M6) are not evaluated at this level, so the reported figure is a ganglionic subscore presented on the 10-point scale. Symmetry-based detection assumes an intact contralateral hemisphere and does not accommodate mass effect, midline shift, bilateral infarction or prior encephalomalacia. Attenuation values derived from 8-bit imagery are estimates, not calibrated Hounsfield units.
Phase 2 Milestone
3D non-rigid deformable atlas registration (ANTs/SyN), supraganglionic slice integration for M4–M6, native DICOM ingestion with rescale-slope handling, and multicenter CT scanner calibration across reconstruction kernels.
